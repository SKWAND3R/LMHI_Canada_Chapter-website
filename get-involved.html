Get Involved

LMHI Canada Chapter relies on contributions to achieve the task at hand — hosting the LMHI World Congress 2027 in Toronto. Your involvement strengthens homeopathy in Canada and supports our mission to advance education, collaboration, and global scientific exchange.

Support the 2027 LMHI World Congress in Toronto

The 2027 LMHI World Homeopathic Congress is coming to Toronto, Canada — bringing together homeopaths, researchers, educators, and healthcare professionals from around the world. This landmark event will showcase the latest advancements in homeopathy, evidence-based research, integrative healthcare, AI, digital health, and clinical innovation.

To successfully host this international congress, the LMHI 2027 Organizing Committee is raising seed funding for essential early-stage planning, including venue deposits, speaker coordination, and student support.

Family and Friends of Homeopathy contributionstrong> — big or small — directly helps make this historic event possible in Canada.

Please contribute securely using the method mentioned below. All contributions are collected and managed directly by the LMHI 2027 Organizing Committee through their official fundraising platform and bank account.
