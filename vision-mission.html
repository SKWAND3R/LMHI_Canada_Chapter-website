Our Mission & Vision

Founded in 2026, LMHI Canada Chapter is a non-profit organization dedicated to promoting health through homeopathy. Our mission and vision reflect our commitment to advancing homeopathic medicine, supporting professional development, and fostering international collaboration.

Mission

Promoting Homeopathy

Organize, host, and support the LMHI World Congress 2027, educational webinars, and official LMHI meetings as may be assigned or approved for the LMHI Canada Chapter.

Collaboration & Liaison

Collaborate and liaise with LMHI and with national and international homeopathic organizations to promote, support, and advance the study, practice, and development of homeopathy in accordance with LMHI principles and ideals in Canada.

Professional Development

Promote academic and professional growth through continuing medical education programs, workshops, lectures, training programs, and scholarly forums related to homeopathy and integrative health.

Public-Benefit Initiatives

Undertake and support public-benefit and educational activities, including awareness programs and outreach initiatives aimed at increasing understanding of homeopathy among professionals and the general public.

Vision

Advance Homeopathy Globally

Promote the advancement and recognition of homeopathy worldwide.

Scientific Exchange

Provide a platform for scientific collaboration, networking, and knowledge sharing.

Uphold LMHI Standards

Maintain high scientific, ethical, and organizational standards in alignment with LMHI’s mission.
