Our Mission & Vision

Founded in 2026, LMHI Canada Chapter is a non-profit organization dedicated to promoting health through homeopathy. Our mission and vision reflect our commitment to advancing homeopathic medicine, supporting professional development, and fostering international collaboration.

Mission

Promoting Homeopathy

Organize, host, and support the LMHI World Congress 2027, educational webinars, and official LMHI meetings.

Collaboration & Liaison

Work with LMHI and international organizations to advance the study and practice of homeopathy in Canada.

Professional Development

Provide continuing education, workshops, lectures, and training programs in homeopathy and integrative health.

Public-Benefit Initiatives

Support awareness programs and outreach initiatives to increase understanding of homeopathy.

Vision

Advance Homeopathy Globally

Promote the advancement and recognition of homeopathy worldwide.

Scientific Exchange

Provide a platform for scientific collaboration, networking, and knowledge sharing.

Uphold LMHI Standards

Maintain high scientific, ethical, and organizational standards in alignment with LMHI’s mission.
